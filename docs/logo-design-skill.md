Read its SKILL.md before logo, wordmark, or letter design.
Read references/typography.md for symbol and text layouts.

## Apply the project rules

Use the business-linked design plan before artwork.
Ask at most three questions per group.
Retain the owner's requested subject and references.
Give familiar product subjects a clear business reason and a distinct form.
Keep the seven brand directions for the existing style comparison.
Use three focused concept comparisons for a selected style when that helps the owner choose the logo format.

The upstream process requests a concept checkpoint before the full kit.
This agrees with the engine's exact-sample approval rule.
Do not treat a repo demo or a test fixture as owner approval.

Preserve the source skill files.
Use the project's STE communication rules in your own replies and reports.
The original skill text and legal notices remain exact source material.

## Available tools

- search_library.py locates reference marks by subject, type, or technique.
- svg_audit.py checks SVG structure and reports possible craft problems.
- concept_sheet.py prepares one concept overview image.
- preview_sheet.py prepares size, background, and comparison tests.
- render_png.py creates PNG files from SVG or HTML sources.
- export_variants.py creates requested variants after the owner chooses a direction.

Use Python on Windows when python3 points to the Store stub.
Run scripts from their exact installed paths.
Inspect generated images before you show them.
The example overview required Chrome because the Inkscape render omitted the filtered artwork.
Use render_png.py with `--backend chrome` when that renderer fault recurs.
Do not change the source skill to hide a renderer fault.

The audit scores are estimates, not proof of production quality.
The example wordmarks contain many anchors because they outline a full serif name.
The cup audit also reports its shallow taper angles.
Those details remain subjects for final craft review after concept selection.
The displayed concepts are drafts, not approved final artwork.

The reference library supports study and comparison only.
Its logos retain their owners' trademark rights.
The MIT license does not grant rights to those reference marks.
