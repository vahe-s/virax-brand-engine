# Brand kit delivery

The owner chooses the required items during the interview.
Create one approved sample before the full kit.
The kit must explain how the logo, type, colors, and voice work together.

## Core files

- A business brief and current brand rules.
- Clean symbol and primary logo SVG files.
- Black and white versions for one-color use.
- A separate SVG with construction guides.
- Editable name treatment and a separate outlined version when required.
- Font files or download sources, with the applicable licenses.
- Color values and permitted text/background pairs.
- A guide for clear space, minimum size, and prohibited changes.
- Brand voice rules and example text based on confirmed business facts.
- Design tokens in JSON and CSS.
- Review records and a manifest of exact file hashes.

## Optional applications

Offer the applications that fit the business:

- Avatar and favicon.
- Business card and letterhead.
- Email signature.
- Social profile and post templates.
- Packaging, signs, or vehicle graphics.
- A website sample with heading, body text, and buttons.

Do not expand the job without a useful business reason.
Complete requested applications with suitable tools and inspect the actual exports.

## Delivery review

1. Open every SVG and check its viewBox and bounds.
2. Inspect artwork at full size and at the intended minimum size.
3. Inspect review boards at 390 px without zoom.
4. Verify symmetry or document the optical corrections.
5. Check font files, glyphs, weights, and licenses.
6. Compare the outlined name with the editable source.
7. Inspect requested PNG and PDF files.
8. Confirm printer requirements and inspect required print proofs.
9. Record the exact delivered file hashes.
10. Get approval for the final kit.

The CLI records sample approval and exports the sample assets.
Its delivery manifest leaves production and final approval checks pending.
It does not perform a vector editor inspection or a print proof.
The agent must retain evidence for any later completed check.

## Media Engine handoff

Copy the approved kit into the business's private Media Engine project.
Give the media agent the profile, rules, fonts, palette, and approved logo files.
Include the kit version and exact review record.
Do not transfer another business's data or account permissions.
Content approval and publication permission remain separate from brand approval.
